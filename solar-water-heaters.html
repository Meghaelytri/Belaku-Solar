Plain or hybrid?

A plain system depends on yesterday's sun. After a cloudy day, the next morning's water often isn't hot enough — and you fall back on a geyser.

A hybrid checks the tank half an hour before your bathing time and heats electrically only if the sun hasn't been enough. Hot water every morning, and it only spends electricity on the days it has to.

Step 1Set it onceYour bathing time and the temperature you want.
Step 2It checks the tankThirty minutes before, automatically.
Step 3Tops up if neededOnly when solar has fallen short.

In hard water, the tank lining decides everything

Water across Mandya is hard. In a plain steel tank, salts settle, scale builds and it eventually leaks. A glass-lined tank is coated inside and out so nothing grips. It costs more and lasts far longer.

Questions about solar water heaters

Straight answers.

Still have questions?
What size solar water heater does my family need?

A 100 LPD system gives 100 litres of hot water a day and comfortably serves three to four people. Five to six people need 150 to 200 LPD. Capacity is counted per day rather than per bath, so if the family also uses hot water in the evening, go one size up.

Do solar water heaters work during the rainy season?

They still heat on overcast days, but not to full temperature. After a heavily clouded day a plain system usually cannot give a hot bath the next morning. A hybrid with a digital controller switches to electric backup by itself, only when solar has not done the job.

How much roof space does a solar water heater need?

A 100 LPD system needs roughly 1.5 by 2 metres of unshaded roof, and space grows by about half a metre of width per additional 100 LPD. The roof must face where it gets clear morning-to-afternoon sun, so we check shade from trees and neighbouring buildings during the site visit.

What maintenance does a solar water heater need?

Very little. The tubes should be checked for scale and cracks roughly once a year, and in hard water areas the tank benefits from periodic flushing. We handle both, and we stock replacement tubes at the shop.

What does a solar water heater cost?

It depends on capacity, tank type and brand. A glass-lined hybrid costs more than a plain ETC system of the same size, and roof access and plumbing distance change the installation cost, so we quote after a site visit.
