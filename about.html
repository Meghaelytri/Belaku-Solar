Who we are

Belaku means light.

Belaku Solar is a family business in Pandavapura, serving Mandya district and Mysuru. We are an authorised dealer and reseller for Nuetech, Aquaguard, Livpure, Purosis, V-Guard, Bajaj, Rally and Luminous — so the warranty you get is the manufacturer's, registered in your name, and claimable through us rather than a helpline.

The shop is a brother concern of Chiguru Furniture And Electronics, run by the same family in the same town. Different trade, same people — and the same reason customers come back.

We deliberately kept this a separate business rather than adding appliances to a furniture shop. Solar water heaters, purifiers and UPS systems need site visits, water tests, load surveys and after-sales service. That is a different job from selling furniture, and it deserved its own name, its own team and its own stock.

How we work

Three things we do differently.

Sizing

We size before we quote

A free site visit, a water test or a load survey comes first. Capacity is decided by your family and your water, not by what we would rather sell.

Installation

Our own technicians

Mounting, plumbing and wiring are done by our team, and we show you how to run the system before we leave.

Service

The same people, afterwards

Filter changes, tube replacements, controller faults and warranty claims come back to us. Common spares are stocked at the shop.

Send us three things

That's all we need to quote properly.

  1. A photoYour roof for solar, the kitchen wall for a purifier, the bathroom for a geyser.
  2. How many people are at homeThis decides capacity more than anything else does.
  3. Your village or areaSo we can confirm we cover it and price the installation properly.

Send us a message for a price.

During shop hours you'll usually have a price back within the hour. No charge for the site visit, and no obligation to buy.
