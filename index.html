Still have questions?
What size solar water heater does my family need?

A 100 LPD system gives 100 litres of hot water a day and comfortably serves three to four people. Five to six people need 150 to 200 LPD. Capacity is counted per day rather than per bath, so if the family also uses hot water in the evening, go one size up.

Do solar water heaters work during the rainy season in Mandya?

They still heat on overcast days, but not to full temperature. After a heavily clouded day a plain system usually cannot give a hot bath the next morning. A hybrid with a digital controller switches to electric backup by itself, only when solar has not done the job.

Which water purifier suits borewell water around Pandavapura?

An RO stage is necessary, because a UV-only purifier does not reduce TDS. We test your water at your home first, and where it carries sand or silt an external sediment pre-filter is fitted so the RO membrane lasts much longer.

Should I choose an on-grid or off-grid rooftop solar system?

Choose on-grid if the goal is a lower bill and your supply is reasonably stable, since there are no batteries and maintenance is lower. Choose off-grid if cuts are frequent and the house must keep running through them. Many homes here are better served by on-grid plus a separate UPS.

What does a solar water heater cost in Pandavapura?

It depends on capacity, tank type and brand. A glass-lined hybrid costs more than a plain ETC system of the same size, and roof access changes the installation cost, so we quote after a site visit.

Who handles service and warranty after installation?

Our own technicians handle filter changes, vacuum tube replacements, controller faults and warranty claims, with common spares stocked at the shop.
